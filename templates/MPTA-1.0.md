# Moral Public Trust Agreement 1.0 (MPTA-1.0)

> **Note:** This is not a legal document. For legal copyright-based protections, check the `LICENSE` file.

1. **User Freedoms:** The user can study, run, build, experiment, extend, share, and ethically commercialize the software.
2. **Local Automation:** Any type of local automation—irrespective of the type of automation, experimentation, research, training, or inferencing—is fully valid, provided it respects human agency, safety, and community trust.
3. **Cloud Automation:** Cloud-based automation is ethically valid **only if** the cloud service provider strictly enforces a zero data retention policy and a zero data harvesting policy.
4. **Unreciprocated Harvesting & License Laundering:** Any type of data harvesting, AI-assisted code translation/rewriting to evade license terms, or model training without reciprocating back to the community (such as open-sourcing derivative logic, sharing weights, or contributing back)—whether legally permitted or not—is ethically invalid.
5. **Open Research & Distillation:** Data harvesting for open research purposes and experimentation is ethically valid, **unless** the resulting non-commercial research model is used for distillation or closed commercialization, in which case it is ethically invalid.
6. **Malicious & Unethical Misuse:** The freedoms granted for experimentation, learning, commercialization, training, or any other use case—whether explicitly stated or not—are ethically valid **unless** used in a malicious, harmful, or deceptive manner that directly or indirectly causes physical or mental harm to humans, environmental damage, or knowingly harms any life form (down to single-celled or bacterial life)—except when strictly conducted with a valid, necessary, or medical justification.

*Published to protect software freedom, individual sovereignty, and community trust.*
