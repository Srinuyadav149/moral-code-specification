# Moral Public Trust Agreement 1.0 (MPTA-1.0)

> **Note:** This is not a legal document. It is a moral declaration. For legal copyright-based protections, check the `LICENSE` file.

1. **User Freedoms:** Studying, running, building, experimenting with, extending, sharing, and commercializing this software is **ethically valid**.
2. **Local Automation:** Any type of local automation irrespective of the type of automation, experimentation, research, training, or inferencing—is **ethically valid**, provided it respects human agency, safety, and community trust.
3. **Cloud Automation:** Cloud-based automation is **ethically valid** *only if* the cloud service provider strictly enforces a zero data retention policy and a zero data harvesting policy.
4. **Unreciprocated Harvesting & License Laundering:** Any type of data harvesting, AI-assisted code translation/rewriting to evade license terms, or model training without reciprocating back to the community (such as open-sourcing derivative logic, sharing weights, or contributing back) whether legally permitted or not is **ethically invalid**.
5. **Open Research & Distillation:** Data harvesting for open research purposes and experimentation is **ethically valid**, *unless* the resulting model is used for distillation or closed commercialization, in which case it is **ethically invalid**.
6. **Malicious & Unethical Misuse:** The freedoms granted for experimentation, learning, commercialization, training, or any other use case—whether explicitly stated or not—are **ethically valid**, *unless* used for harmful, deceptive, or destructive purposes. Specifically:
   * **Human Safety:** Causing direct or indirect physical or mental harm to humans, or violating fundamental human rights, is **ethically invalid**.
   * **Biosphere & Progress:** Causing damage to the biosphere, or prioritizing the speed of technological progress over environmental preservation when the two conflict, is **ethically invalid**.
   * **Harm to Life:** Knowingly harming or destroying any living organism—regardless of scale or perceived significance—without clear justification rooted in sanitation, hygiene, or medical necessity, is **ethically invalid**.
   * **Animal Stewardship:** Deploying automated or commercial systems that treat animals purely as resources without ensuring humane treatment and an improved quality of life is **ethically invalid**.
